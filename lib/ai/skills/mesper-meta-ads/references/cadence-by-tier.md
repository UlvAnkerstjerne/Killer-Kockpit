## Interpretation for MESPER

- Phase-1 accounts (Cold-Start): target the Top-25 % pace, not the median
- Phase-3 accounts (Scaling): cadence may slow, but winner refresh discipline is mandatory
- Tier transitions (e.g. Small → Medium): increase cadence proactively, otherwise winners become rare
