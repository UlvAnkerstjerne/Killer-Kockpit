# Hit-Rate Benchmarks by Vertical

Source: Motion Creative Benchmarks Report 2026.
Hit rate = winner ads ÷ total ads per account (unweighted mean within tier).
Winner definition: ≥10× account median spend AND ≥$500 absolute.

## Hit Rate per Spend Tier (all verticals averaged)

| Tier | Spend / month | Avg hit rate | Avg creatives / week |
|---|---|---|---|
| Micro | <$10k | 4.0 % | 2.8 |
| Small | $10k–$50k | 6.4 % | 4.1 |
| Medium | $50k–$200k | 8.1 % | 6.6 |
| Large | $200k–$1M | 8.6 % | 11.2 |
| Enterprise | >$1M | 8.8 % | 18.8 |
